# Cruit Lab — Compliance Notes (NCAA)

> ⚠️ This is a plain-language summary of public NCAA materials for planning
> purposes — **not legal or compliance advice.** Georgia State's compliance
> office is the only authority that counts, and NCAA bylaws change. Jon must get

---

## Why this matters

Jon is an active **graduate assistant football coach at Georgia State (NCAA
Division I)**. Football coaches — including GAs — are a specially restricted
category. Brax (Auburn grad, not an athlete or staff) is unrestricted and
handles administration.

Because the business is fundamentally Jon's football venture, the core activity
("a D1 football coach runs a private football training/recruiting business") is
exactly what NCAA rules scrutinize. The path forward is **disclosure +
approval**, plus structuring the launch around what's permitted now.

---

## What the rules say (with sources)

1. **GA coaches can't evaluate/contact prospects off-campus** — "may not
   evaluate or contact prospective student-athletes off campus, regardless of
   whether compensation is received."
   [NCAA via UGA Compliance](https://compliance.sports.uga.edu/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Football-Graduate-Assistant-Rule.pdf)

2. **Football coaches can't work privately-owned camps/clinics** — "not
   permissible for a football coach... with responsibilities specific to
   football to be employed at a noninstitutional, privately owned camp or
   clinic." (Football is carved out from the private-lessons allowance other
   sports get.)
   [NCAA camp regs](https://presbyterianc_ftp.sidearmsports.com/custompages/Compliance%20Manuals/10.%20Summer%20Camps,%20Outside%20Competition%20&%20Local%20Sports%20Club.pdf)

3. **Outside athletically-related income must be disclosed & approved** — must
   be reported to and approved by the institution's president/chancellor
   (often via the AD), annually. Income is "athletically related" if it ties to
   his reputation/ability or his employment at the school.
   [Bylaw 11.2.2 summary](https://www.bsk.com/higher-education-law-report/new-ncaa-legislation-necessitates-changes-to-institutional-employment-agreements)

4. **GA recruiting role is limited even on-campus** — on-campus recruiting only
   after passing the coaches' certification exam; off-campus prohibited.
   [NCAA.com](https://www.ncaa.com/news/ncaa/article/2023-01-11/ncaa-division-i-council-modernizes-rules-coaching-limits)

**Note on "prospect":** A prospective student-athlete (PSA) is generally a
student who has started 9th grade. Youth players below that and adult players
are typically **not** PSAs — which is why the youth/adult/education market
carries less recruiting-rule exposure.

## The operating structure (clean, no evasion)

- **Brax** owns/operates the business and handles all restricted, client-facing
  work (prospect evaluations, recruiting consults, sales, billing).
- **Jon** contributes **approved** evergreen products: Student-of-the-Game
  curriculum, NFL/college film breakdowns, drill/technique design — built as
  business assets, not live service delivery to prospects.
- **Target non-PSAs first** (youth, adults, general football education) to
  minimize recruiting-rule exposure.
- **No Georgia State name, logo, title, facilities, or staff time** used to
  promote Cruit Lab.
- **Disclose everything** Jon does and earns to GSU compliance and get written
  approval. Transparency is the protection — do **not** hide his involvement or
  route his income to disguise it.

## Timeline strategy

GA roles are temporary (~1–2 years). Build everything non-restricted **now**
(entity, brand, website, payments, content library, approved curriculum), then
expand to the full model — including the recruiting tier and Jon's public,
paid involvement — when his status changes or compliance clears it.

## Questions for Jon to bring to Georgia State Compliance

Bring these for clear, written answers in one meeting:

1. Can I have an ownership interest in / operate a private football
   training/education business while a football GA? Any limits?
2. The business will be operated and client-facing by a non-staff partner
   (Brax). Does that change what I'm permitted to do?
3. Can I create and sell **educational content** (film-study courses, football
   concept/IQ material, NFL/college tape breakdowns) for compensation?
4. Can I design drills/curriculum used by the business if I'm not personally
   delivering them at a camp/clinic?
5. Where's the line on working with **non-prospects** (pre-9th-grade youth,
   adult-league players) vs. PSAs?
6. What exactly counts as prohibited "evaluation" of a prospect — does a paid
   film review of a high schooler's game tape fall under it?
7. What's GSU's process and form for disclosing/approving outside
   athletically-related income? What's the dollar threshold and timing?
8. Are there restrictions on using "Division I coaching experience" (without the
   school's name/logo) in marketing?
9. Can my name/likeness appear in the business's content, or should I stay
   uncredited for now?
10. Is there anything about this that could affect my eligibility for future
    coaching roles or the program's compliance standing?
