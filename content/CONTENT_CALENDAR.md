## The 4 content pillars
Rotate these so the feed stays varied and every follower type is served:

1. **Teach the Game** (IQ) — coverages, concepts, "why" behind plays. *Your differentiator.*
2. **Technique** — WR releases, route detail, footwork, hands.
3. **Honest Take** — real recruiting truth parents don't hear elsewhere. *Your brand.*
4. **Tape Breakdown** — analyze NFL/college WR reps (Diggs, Chase, etc.).

## Production notes (keep it simple)
- **Gear:** a phone + a telestration app (Hudl Technique / CoachTube) for the tape breakdowns; a clip mic helps.
- **Format:** hook in the first 2 seconds, one idea per video, 20–45 seconds.
- **Captions:** always add on-screen text — most people watch muted.
- **Consistency > polish.** Posting 4x/week beats a perfect video once a month.
- **Batch film:** shoot a week (or month) of talking-head clips in one session.

## What to track
Simple sheet: date, pillar, platform, views, saves, comments, profile visits,
DMs/leads. After 2–3 weeks, **double down on the pillar that drives the most
profile visits and DMs** — that's your lead engine.
