## 2. Honest target list *(from the Tier Rubric)*
Build a balanced list — not all reaches.

| Category | # of schools | Examples (fill in) |
|---|---|---|
| **Reach** (top of your range) | 3–5 | |
| **Target** (right in your range) | 6–10 | |
| **Safety** (you'd clearly contribute) | 4–6 | |

For each school, note: **fit** (scheme/position need), **academics fit**,
**roster/depth-chart reality**, and **why you'd actually go there.**

---

## 3. Outreach plan *(the right way)*
- [ ] Build the Hudl profile + highlight reel (best plays first, 2–3 min).
- [ ] Register with the **NCAA Eligibility Center** (for D1/D2).
- [ ] Draft a personalized intro email template (see below).
- [ ] Identify the right coach to contact per school (position coach / recruiting coordinator).
- [ ] Track every contact in a spreadsheet (school, coach, date, response).
- [ ] Follow up after each game with updated film/stats.

### Intro email template
> **Subject:** [Position] | [Grad Year] | [Name] — [Height/Weight], [GPA], [40 time]
>
> Coach [Name],
>
> My name is [Name], a [position] in the class of [year] at [school]. I'm very
> interested in [School] because [specific, real reason]. Here's my film: [link].
> Measurables: [H/W, 40, GPA, test]. I'd love to learn what you look for at my
> position and how I can earn a look. Thank you for your time.
>
> [Name] · [phone] · [coach contact]

---

## 4. Timeline by grade
- **Freshman/Soph:** train, get film, build grades, learn the process.
- **Junior:** outreach begins, camps, verified testing, narrow the list.
- **Senior:** finalize, official visits, decisions, signing.

> Earlier is better. Grades and film compound over time.

---

## 5. Honesty checkpoints
- ☐ Is this target list realistic per the Tier Rubric — or wishful?
- ☐ Are grades on track for the schools on the list?
- ☐ Is the film actually showing the player at the projected level?
- ☐ Have we told the family the truth about the range?
