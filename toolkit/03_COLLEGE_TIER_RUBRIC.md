# Cruit Lab — College Tier Assessment Rubric

> The honest framework for answering "where can I actually play?" Use it with the
> Film Evaluation so the level read is consistent and defensible — not a guess.
>
> ⚠️ These are **general scouting guidelines, not guarantees.** Recruiting depends
> on film, position, scheme fit, timing, grades, and luck. Always frame as a
> realistic *range*. (See `COMPLIANCE_NOTES.md` — while Jon is a GA, prospect
> evaluation/recruiting advice is Brax's lane.)

---

## How to use it
1. Confirm the player's measurables and grades from the intake form.
2. Compare honestly against the tier benchmarks below.
3. Weight **film over measurables** — coaches recruit players, not numbers.
4. Land on a **realistic range** (e.g., "D2/D3 now, D1 FCS possible with a
   verified 40 and a stronger junior season").

---

## The levels at a glance

| Tier | What it is | General reality |
|---|---|---|
| **D1 FBS** | Top level (Power conferences + Group of 5) | Elite size/speed/film; heavily recruited; rare |
| **D1 FCS** | Division I, scholarship, a step below FBS | Strong athletes; very competitive |
| **D2** | Scholarship (often partial) | Solid athletes; productive HS starters |
| **D3** | No athletic scholarships (academic/need aid) | Good players; academics matter a lot |
| **NAIA** | Smaller colleges, scholarships available | Comparable to D2/low-D1 range; underrated path |
| **JUCO** | Two-year; a development/redemption route | Path to 4-yr for raw, late, or academic-risk players |

## Honest benchmarks (WR example)
*Guidelines only — adjust per position. "Play speed" beats "timed speed."*

| Trait | D1 FBS | D1 FCS / D2 | D3 / NAIA | JUCO route |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Height | 6'0"+ (or elite traits) | 5'10"+ | varies | varies |
| 40 (verified) | ~4.4–4.5 | ~4.5–4.6 | ~4.6–4.7+ | raw, projectable |
| Production | dominant vs. good comp | clear starter | solid starter | flashes, inconsistent |
| Film IQ | advanced | good | developing | raw |
| Separation | elite | good | average+ | inconsistent |

> comparison*, not exact cutoffs — a 6'3" WR with a 4.7 and great hands/IQ can
> outrank a 4.5 with no route detail.

## The grades gate (don't skip this)
Talent gets you looked at; grades decide your *options*.

- **Strong GPA + test scores** → opens **D3 academic aid**, **Ivy/Patriot**,
  and makes you a safer bet everywhere.
- **NCAA eligibility:** must complete the required core courses and register
  with the **NCAA Eligibility Center** for D1/D2.
- **At-risk grades** → JUCO or D3/NAIA may be the realistic path; say so.

## The Cruit Lab "honest read" script
Use this structure when you deliver the verdict — direct, but constructive:

1. **Where you are now:** "Based on the film, you're a realistic **[tier]** player today."
2. **The range:** "Your ceiling with development is **[tier]**; your floor is **[tier]**."
3. **The gap:** "The one thing standing between you and the next level is **[X]**."
4. **The plan:** "Here's exactly what we'd work on to push your range up."
5. **The path:** "These are the *types* of programs that fit your film, size, and grades."

> Never inflate. The brand is built on being the person who told the truth.

---

## Fit factors beyond talent
- **Scheme fit** — a slot-only WR fits some offenses, not others.
- **Position value & need** — depth charts and class needs change everything.
- **Timing** — start outreach early (sophomore/junior year).
- **Exposure** — verified testing, camps, quality film, coachable reputation.
- **Character & academics** — coaches recruit people they trust.
