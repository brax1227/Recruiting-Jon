# Cruit Lab — Business Plan

> **Cruit Lab** — the honest football lab. A one-stop shop for player
> development and honest recruiting guidance: film evaluation, position
> technique, football IQ, and realistic college placement.
>
> Brand details (name, tagline, voice, visual direction) live in
> [`BRAND_IDENTITY.md`](./BRAND_IDENTITY.md).

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This business provides personalized football evaluation, wide receiver
development, and honest recruiting consultation for youth, high school, and
amateur players — and for the parents and coaches around them.

The core advantage is **honesty plus depth**. Most trainers teach physical
skills only and most recruiting services sugarcoat a player's level. This
business does both the mental and physical sides of the game *and* tells
families the truth about where a player can realistically play and thrive.

**Founder edge:** a playing/coaching background — including time on a college
roster — gives firsthand knowledge of what it actually takes to get recruited
and contribute at each level.

**Revenue model:** a five-tier ladder from a one-time film evaluation up to a
full monthly development package, with a separate recruiting consultation
track.

**Realistic target:** 5 full-package clients plus a few recruiting consults =
roughly **$2,000–$4,500/month**. Scaling to ~10 clients puts the business at
**$5,000+/month**.

---

## 2. The Opportunity

Youth and high school football is a large, emotionally invested market.
Families spend heavily on camps, trainers, and exposure — but the guidance
they receive is often:

- **Generic** — drills with no individualized feedback.
- **Physical-only** — footwork and routes, but no football IQ.
- **Dishonest** — everyone is told they're a "D1 prospect" to keep them paying.

That creates a clear gap: a single trusted source that develops the whole
player (body *and* mind) and gives families a straight answer about their real
recruiting ceiling. This is a legitimate and growing niche.

## 4. Services & Pricing

A five-tier ladder. Lower tiers are one-time entry points; higher tiers are
recurring revenue with high retention.

| Tier | Service | Price |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Film Evaluation | $50–$150 (one-time) |
| 2 | WR School | $150–$300 / month |
| 3 | Student of the Game | $200–$400 / month |
| 4 | Full Development Package | $400–$700 / month |
| 5 | Recruiting Consultation | $100–$300 one-time *or* $300+/month |

### Tier 1 — Film Evaluation ($50–$150)
- Personalized breakdown of the player's game footage.
- Written report covering strengths and weaknesses.
- Position-specific tips.
- One-time purchase. **This is the front door** — the low-cost offer that
  brings clients in and proves value before they commit monthly.

### Tier 2 — WR School ($150–$300/month)
- Position-specific drills and technique.
- Route running, releases, hands, footwork.
- Virtual or in-person sessions.
- Weekly feedback on submitted clips.

### Tier 3 — Student of the Game ($200–$400/month) — *the differentiator*
- Passing concepts and route combinations.
- Defensive fronts and how to attack them.
- Coverage recognition (Cover 1, 2, 3, 4, 6).
- Run schemes and blocking assignments.
- Film study sessions — breaking down NFL/college tape.
- Turns casual players into football-IQ guys.

> **Why this tier is a difference maker:** Most WR trainers only teach physical
> skills. Very few teach the mental game. This develops players that coaches
> actually want — players who understand football, not just run routes.

### Tier 4 — Full Development Package ($400–$700/month)
- Everything in Tiers 2 and 3 combined.
- Best value, best results, highest retention.
- The flagship offer — the goal is to move serious clients here.

### Tier 5 — Recruiting Consultation ($100–$300 one-time, or $300+/month)
- Honest assessment of the player's current level.
- Realistic college-tier breakdown: D1 (FBS/FCS), D2, D3, NAIA, JUCO.
- Which schools fit their size, speed, grades, and film.
- How to reach out to coaches the right way.
- Transcript and GPA guidance.
- Building their recruiting profile.

## 5. The Brand: "The Honest Factor"

The brand position is **the guy who tells the truth.** Lean into it hard:

> "I'm not going to tell your son he's a D1 prospect if he's not. I'm going to
> tell you exactly where he can play, get on the field, and thrive."

Why this works:
- Parents are desperate for honest guidance and rarely get it.
- Most recruiters sugarcoat everything.
- Firsthand college-roster experience makes the assessment credible.
- It saves families from chasing the wrong schools and wasting time and money.

Honesty is the moat. It's rare, it builds trust, and trust drives referrals.

## 6. Go-to-Market Strategy

### Launch sequence
1. **Do 2–3 free evaluations** to build sample work and testimonials.
2. **Post breakdown clips** on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
3. **Join youth football Facebook groups** and offer the service.
4. **Partner with a local coach or trainer** for referrals.

### Funnel logic
- Free/low-cost **Film Evaluation** is the top of the funnel.
- Deliver obvious value → convert to a monthly tier.
- Push toward the **Full Development Package** for retention.
- **Recruiting Consultation** captures HS families at decision time and is a
  natural cross-sell.

## 7. Content & Marketing Strategy

Short-form educational content doubles as marketing and proof of expertise.

Starter content ideas:
- "What is Cover 2 and how do you beat it?" — short explainer video.
- Breaking down a Stefon Diggs or Ja'Marr Chase route.
- "3 releases every WR needs" — Reels/TikTok.
- Film-evaluation snippets (with permission) showing the breakdown process.
- "Honest take" series — the realistic recruiting truth parents won't hear
  elsewhere.

Goal: every piece of content demonstrates the **Student of the Game** depth and
the **honest** brand at the same time.

## 8. Operations & Tools

What's needed to run it:
- **Football knowledge** — playing/coaching background (already a strength).
- **Video annotation** — Hudl Technique or Coach's Eye for telestration.
- **Basic editing + a phone** — enough to produce reports and social clips.
- **Delivery formats** — written reports (PDF), annotated video breakdowns,
  and live virtual sessions.

Future operational needs as it scales:
- Standardized report templates and a recruiting-profile worksheet.
- A booking/scheduling system and a simple intake form.
- Payment processing for one-time and recurring billing.
