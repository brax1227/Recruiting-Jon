# Cruit Lab — Brand Identity

> The honest football lab: where players get developed, evaluated, and
> recruited — for real.

---

## 1. The Name

**Cruit Lab**

- **"Cruit"** — short for *recruit*. Instantly signals the recruiting core that
  parents and players are emotionally invested in. Distinctive spelling makes it
  ownable (trademark + domain + handles).
- **"Lab"** — development, experimentation, the *science* of building a player.
  Covers the training and football-IQ half of the business and keeps the name
  position-agnostic (works for any position, not just WR).

**Styling**
- Primary: **Cruit Lab**
- Handle/domain: **CruitLab** → `cruitlab.com`, `@cruitlab`
- Logo stylization: **CRUIT LAB** or **Cruit Lab.** (period as a "final word /
  honest" punctuation mark)

**Trademark note (preliminary, not legal advice):** No exact "Cruit Lab" mark
or brand was found in the sports/recruiting space. One adjacent name exists —
"The Recruit Lab" — but it operates in *employment* recruiting, a different
industry, under a different name. Recommended before filing: run the name
through the official USPTO search (tmsearch.uspto.gov), confirm domain/handles,
and consider a flat-fee trademark filing.

## 3. Positioning

**One-line positioning**
> Cruit Lab is the honest football lab that develops the whole player — body and
> football IQ — and tells families the truth about where they can actually play.

**The problem we solve**
- Most trainers teach physical skills only — no football IQ.
- Most recruiting services sugarcoat everything to keep families paying.
- Parents are desperate for honest guidance and rarely get it.

**Our answer**
- Whole-player development: technique *and* the mental game.
- Honest, realistic recruiting assessments grounded in college-roster
  experience.
- One place for film, technique, IQ, and college placement.

**The moat:** honesty. It's rare, it builds trust, and trust drives referrals.

---

## 4. Brand Voice

**Personality:** Straight-up. Knowledgeable. Player-to-player real. A coach who
respects you enough to tell you the truth.

**We sound like**
- Direct and honest — no hype, no sugarcoating.
- Confident from experience, not arrogant.
- Encouraging but realistic — "here's where you can win."
- Smart about the game without being condescending.

**We don't sound like**
- A hype machine promising everyone is a D1 star.
- A faceless recruiting service.
- A drill-only trainer with no substance.

**Signature line (brand voice in one quote):**
> "I'm not going to tell your son he's a D1 prospect if he's not. I'm going to
> tell you exactly where he can play, get on the field, and thrive."

## 5. Visual Direction

**Concept:** "The Lab" — a clean, modern training-science aesthetic with an
edge. Football grit meets blueprint/experiment precision. Subtle anime energy in
the supporting graphics (a level-up / EXP-bar motif, "break your ceiling"
progress visuals) without making the core mark cartoonish.

**Logo direction**
- Wordmark-forward: **CRUIT LAB** in a bold, athletic sans-serif.
- Optional mark: a flask/beaker silhouette formed from a football, or a "CL"
  monogram with a lab-flask negative space.
- Keep it clean enough to read at handle/avatar size.

**Color direction** (pick one lane in design)
- **Lab/clean:** deep navy + electric/clinical green/teal accent + white.
- **Grit/edge:** matte black + a single high-energy accent (volt green or
  bright orange) + concrete gray.
- The accent color should pop on dark for social clips and merch.

**Typography**
- Headlines: bold condensed athletic sans (jersey/scoreboard energy).
- Body: clean readable sans for reports and the site.

**Graphic motifs**
- EXP/level-up progress bars ("Player Level: Unlocked").
- Blueprint/telestration lines (routes, coverage diagrams) as texture.
- "Real talk" stamp/badge for the honesty content series.
